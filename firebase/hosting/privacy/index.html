2. Information we collect

Account information

Your mobile phone number, which is how you sign in. To verify it, our service provider Google (Firebase Authentication) sends you a one-time code by SMS. We also record when you agreed to these terms.

Profile information

Your display name, optional about text and optional profile photo. These are visible to people you chat with and to members of groups you are in. Your profile photo is hidden from people you have blocked. Profile photos are processed on our servers to remove hidden information such as location.

Your contacts

Only if you allow it, Samvad reads the phone numbers in your address book and checks them against Samvad accounts so you can see which contacts use Samvad. The numbers are sent over an encrypted connection, compared, and not stored on our servers. Contact names are read and kept only on your device, to show people under the names you saved; they are never uploaded.

Messages and content

The text messages, photos, voice messages, documents, polls, reactions, replies, edits and other content you send, along with the time sent and delivery and read status. This content is stored on our servers so it can be delivered and synced to your devices. Photos you send are resized and have hidden information such as location removed on your device before upload.

Starred messages (a reference to the message you starred) and scheduled messages (the text, chat and send time, until sent and for 7 days after) are stored so they work across your devices.

Groups

Group name, description, photo, members, admins, when members joined, and invite codes. Group events (such as "Asha added Ravi") are visible to members.

Activity and presence

Whether you are online, when you were last seen, and whether you are typing in a chat. You can hide your last seen in Settings. If you turn read receipts off, others do not see when you read their messages.

Device and technical information

A random identifier for each install, the app version, the Android version, and a push notification token so we can deliver notifications. We use Google Play Integrity (Firebase App Check) to confirm requests come from the genuine Samvad app. Our servers keep technical logs to run and secure the service; these logs are designed not to contain message content or phone numbers.

Security information

If you turn on two-step verification, we store your PIN only as a salted, one-way hash (scrypt), together with failed-attempt counts and lockout times. App lock and screenshot blocking work on your device and do not send us any biometric data.

Safety reports and blocks

If you report someone, the report includes the reported account and the last 5 messages of that chat, so we can review it. If you block someone, we store that so their messages and presence are blocked.

Information kept only on your device

Chat themes, wallpaper photos you choose, saved-contact names, app lock and other preferences stay on your device and are not uploaded. Exported chats are files you create and control.

3. What we do not do

  • We do not sell, rent or trade your personal data.
  • We do not show advertising or use advertising, analytics or tracking SDKs.
  • We do not collect your location.
  • We do not read your contacts' names on our servers or build profiles of people who are not Samvad users.
  • We do not use your messages to train artificial intelligence models.

5. Who can see or receive your information

  • People you chat with see your profile, the messages you send them, your presence (unless hidden) and read status (unless turned off). Group members see your profile and messages in that group from when they joined.
  • Our service providers. We use Google Cloud and Firebase (Google) to host our databases, files and servers, send sign-in SMS codes, deliver push notifications and check app integrity. Google processes this data on our behalf under its data processing terms.
  • Authorities. We disclose information when required by Indian law, a court order or a valid request from a government agency, for example under the Information Technology Act, 2000 and the rules made under it, and to report child sexual abuse material.
  • Business changes. If Samvad is merged, acquired or reorganised, your information may move to the new owner, who must keep protecting it as this policy describes. We will tell you before that happens.

6. Where your information is stored

Our main database, files and servers are in Google Cloud's Mumbai, India region. Online status and typing information is kept in Google Cloud's Singapore region. Our service providers may process data in other countries to provide their services. Any transfer outside India is made in line with the DPDP Act and the restrictions the Government of India may notify.

8. How we protect it

  • All connections are encrypted (TLS), and data is encrypted while stored.
  • Strict access rules on our servers check every request, so only the people in a chat can read it.
  • App integrity checks block requests that do not come from the genuine app.
  • Optional two-step verification, app lock, screenshot blocking and private notifications.
  • Limits on sign-in attempts, contact lookups, reports and PIN guesses to prevent abuse.

Messages are not yet end-to-end encrypted. We will update this policy and tell you in the app when end-to-end encryption is available. No system is perfectly secure. If a personal data breach occurs, we will inform the Data Protection Board of India and affected users as the DPDP Act requires.

9. Your rights

Under the DPDP Act you have the right to:

  • Access a summary of your personal data we process and the processing activities, and the identities of those we have shared it with.
  • Correct, complete and update your data. You can change your name, about and photo in the app at any time.
  • Erase your data by deleting your account in the app, or by asking us (see Delete your account).
  • Withdraw consent at any time, as easily as you gave it.
  • Grievance redressal: complain to our Grievance Officer (section 12), and if you are not satisfied, to the Data Protection Board of India.
  • Nominate another person to exercise these rights if you die or become unable to.

To use any of these rights, email {{GRIEVANCE_EMAIL}} and include your registered phone number. We will ask you to confirm the request from your registered phone number so no one else can act for you, and we respond within the time the law requires.

10. Children

Samvad is for people aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly process the personal data of children (people under 18). If we learn that a child has created an account, we will delete it. If you believe a child is using Samvad, contact us. Our Child Safety Standards describe how we act against child sexual abuse and exploitation.
