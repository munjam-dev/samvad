Child Safety Standards

Effective 14 October 2026 · Samvad, by {{OPERATOR}}

Samvad has zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE), including child sexual abuse material (CSAM), grooming, sextortion and trafficking.

Our standards

  • Samvad is only for people aged 18 and over. Accounts of children are deleted when we become aware of them.
  • Sharing, requesting, producing or promoting CSAM, sexualising minors, grooming, or any other exploitation of children is prohibited by our Terms of Service.
  • We do not allow content or behaviour that endangers children, even when it does not break a specific law.

How to report

  • In the app: open the chat, tap the ⋮ menu, then Report. The report reaches our safety team with the last 5 messages of that chat. You can also block the person.
  • By email: {{CHILD_SAFETY_EMAIL}}, our designated child safety contact. Please do not attach or forward the abusive images themselves; describe what you saw and include the phone number or group involved.
  • If a child is in immediate danger, call 112 (India emergency) or 1098 (CHILDLINE India), and report online at cybercrime.gov.in.

What we do

  • Review CSAE reports as our highest priority, and act on content showing child sexual abuse within 24 hours.
  • Remove the content and permanently disable the accounts involved, which also stops their phone numbers from signing in to Samvad again.
  • Preserve the relevant information and report it to the appropriate authorities, including Indian law enforcement through the National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal, and the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) where applicable, as required by law, including the Protection of Children from Sexual Offences (POCSO) Act, 2012 and the Information Technology Act, 2000.
  • Cooperate fully with lawful requests from authorities investigating child exploitation.

Compliance

Samvad complies with applicable child safety laws in India and in the countries where it is available. Our designated point of contact for child safety matters is reachable at {{CHILD_SAFETY_EMAIL}}.
