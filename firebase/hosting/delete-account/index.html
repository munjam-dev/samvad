Delete your account

Samvad, by {{OPERATOR}}

In the app (fastest)

  1. Open Samvad and tap Settings.
  2. Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete account.
  3. Confirm. For your safety you will be asked to verify again with a one-time SMS code, or your two-step PIN if it is turned on.

Deletion starts straight away and finishes within a few minutes.

Without the app

If you no longer have the app or your phone, email {{GRIEVANCE_EMAIL}} with the subject "Delete my Samvad account" and your registered phone number (with country code). To make sure it is really you, we will ask you to confirm from that number. We complete verified requests within 7 days and email you when it is done.

What is deleted

  • Your account and phone number sign-in
  • Your profile: name, about and profile photo
  • Your devices, notification tokens and settings stored on our servers
  • Your starred messages and all your scheduled messages
  • Your blocks, two-step verification data and online / last seen status
  • Your membership of every chat and group. If you were a group's only admin, the longest-standing member becomes admin.

What is kept, and for how long

  • Messages you already sent stay in the other people's chats, because they are part of their conversation history. They show your name as a deleted account.
  • Safety reports about your account or made by you are kept for up to 90 days, unless the law requires longer.
  • Technical logs (which contain no message content or phone numbers) expire within 30 days.
  • A record that the account was deleted, with no profile or messages, so the old account cannot be misused.
  • Information we must keep to comply with a law, court order or investigation, only for as long as required.

Things to know first

  • Deletion cannot be undone. If you want a copy of a chat, use Export chat in that chat's menu before deleting.
  • Data stored only on your phone (chat themes, wallpaper photos, saved names) is removed when you sign out or uninstall the app.
  • You can create a new account with the same number later; it will start empty.
