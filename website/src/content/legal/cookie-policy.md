> **In short.** This website does not use advertising, analytics or tracking cookies, and does not share your browsing with anyone. It only remembers your light or dark theme choice on your own device.

## 1. What cookies and similar technologies are

Cookies are small files a website saves in your browser. Similar technologies, such as a browser's local storage, let a site remember information on your device.

## 2. What this website uses

| Name | Type | Purpose | How long |
|---|---|---|---|
| `theme` | Local storage | Remembers whether you chose the light or dark theme | Until you clear it |

That is all. We do **not** use:

- analytics or measurement tools (such as Google Analytics);
- advertising, retargeting or social media pixels;
- fingerprinting or cross-site tracking.

Because we only use what is strictly necessary for the site to work as you asked, we do not show a cookie consent banner.

## 3. Server logs

Like every website, our hosting provider (Google Firebase Hosting) records basic technical information when you visit — such as your IP address, the page requested, the date and time, and your browser type — to deliver the site and protect it from abuse. These logs are kept for a short period and are not used to track you. See our [Privacy Policy](/legal/privacy-policy).

## 4. The Samvad app

The Samvad app does not use cookies or advertising identifiers. How the app handles data is explained in our [Privacy Policy](/legal/privacy-policy).

## 5. Managing storage

You can clear this site's stored data at any time in your browser's settings (usually under *Privacy* → *Site data*). The site will simply return to your device's default theme.

## 6. Changes

If we ever add a cookie that is not strictly necessary, we will update this policy first and ask for your consent before setting it.
