> **In short.** Delete your account any time from **Settings → Delete account** in the Samvad app. If you no longer have the app, email us and we will do it for you after confirming it is really you. Deletion is permanent.

## 1. Delete your account in the app

1. Open **Samvad** and tap **Settings**.
2. Scroll down and tap **Delete account**.
3. Read what will happen, then tap **Delete my account**.
4. For your safety, confirm with a new one-time code sent by SMS — or your **two-step PIN** if you have turned two-step verification on.

Deletion starts straight away and finishes within a few minutes. You are signed out on all your devices.

## 2. Delete your account without the app

If you have lost your phone, uninstalled the app, or cannot sign in:

1. Email [{{GRIEVANCE_EMAIL}}](mailto:{{GRIEVANCE_EMAIL}}) with the subject **"Delete my Samvad account"**.
2. Include the **phone number** of the account, with country code (for example, +91 98765 43210).
3. We will reply asking you to confirm that the request comes from you, using the phone number on the account. We never ask for your sign-in code or PIN.
4. We complete verified requests within **7 days** and email you when it is done.

## 3. Ask us to delete some data, but keep your account

You can delete much of your data yourself, without deleting your account:

| Data | How to delete it |
|---|---|
| A message you sent | Touch and hold it → **Delete for everyone** |
| Your profile name, about or photo | Settings → edit your profile |
| A starred message | Touch and hold it → **Unstar**, or remove it from **Starred messages** |
| A scheduled message | Open the chat → tap the scheduled banner → **Cancel** |
| Your membership of a group | Group info → **Leave group** |
| Two-step verification data | Settings → Two-step verification → turn it off |
| Data stored only on your phone (themes, wallpapers, saved names, search index, Personal Vault) | Sign out, or uninstall the app |

For anything else, email [{{GRIEVANCE_EMAIL}}](mailto:{{GRIEVANCE_EMAIL}}) and tell us what you would like deleted. We respond within 30 days.

## 4. What is deleted

When your account is deleted, we delete:

- your account and phone-number sign-in;
- your profile: display name, about text and profile photo;
- your registered devices, notification tokens and settings stored on our servers;
- your starred messages and all your scheduled messages;
- your blocks, two-step verification data, and online / last seen status;
- your linked-device records and your appeals;
- any live call you are in (it is ended) and your place in meetings;
- your membership of every chat and group. If you were a group's only admin, its longest-standing member becomes admin.

## 5. What is kept, and why

| Data | Kept for | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Messages you already sent | As long as the people you sent them to keep them | They are part of other people's conversation history. They show as from a deleted account |
| Call records (who called whom, when, how long) | Until the other person deletes theirs or their account | They are part of the other person's call history |
| Your Moments | Expire 24 hours after posting | Moments are time-limited |
| Safety reports about you or made by you | Up to 90 days | To finish reviewing abuse, unless the law requires longer |
| Server logs (no message content or phone numbers) | Up to 30 days | Security and troubleshooting |
| A record that the account was deleted, with no profile or messages | Kept | So a deleted account cannot be misused |
| Data we must keep under a law, court order or investigation | Only as long as required | Legal obligation |

## 6. Before you delete

- **Your Personal Vault is on your phone only and has no backup.** Uninstalling the app or clearing its data erases it for good. Export anything you want to keep first.
- **Deletion cannot be undone.** You cannot recover your account, profile or chats afterwards.
- **Want a copy?** Use **Export chat** from a chat's menu to save it before you delete.
- **Changing your number?** Delete your account before your old number is given to someone else.
- **Starting again?** You can create a new account with the same number later. It will start empty.
