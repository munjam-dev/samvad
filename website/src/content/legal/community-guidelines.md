> **In short.** Samvad is for real conversations between people who want to talk to each other. Be respectful, be honest, respect privacy, and keep children safe. These guidelines are part of our [Terms of Service](/legal/terms-of-service); breaking them can lead to removal of content or loss of your account.

## 1. Why these guidelines exist

Samvad means *dialogue*. We want every conversation on Samvad to be one that both sides chose to have. These guidelines explain, in plain language and with examples, what that means in practice. They apply to everything you send: messages, photos, voice notes, files, polls, profile names, about text, profile and group photos, and group names and descriptions.

## 2. Respect people

**Not allowed:**

- Threats of violence, or encouraging others to harm someone.
- Harassment, bullying, stalking, or repeatedly contacting someone who has blocked you or asked you to stop, including by creating new accounts.
- Hate speech: attacking people for their religion, caste, race, ethnicity, nationality, gender, sexual orientation, disability or serious illness.
- Sharing someone's sexual or intimate images without their consent ("revenge porn"), or threatening to.
- Morphed, deepfake or edited images or audio that put someone in a sexual, humiliating or false situation.

**Allowed:** disagreeing strongly and respectfully, discussing difficult topics, reporting news, and sharing your own experiences.

## 3. Keep children safe

Samvad is for adults only. We have **zero tolerance** for anything that sexualises or exploits children, including child sexual abuse material, grooming, sextortion and trafficking. We remove it, close the accounts involved, and report it to the authorities. See our [Child Safety Standards](/legal/child-safety-standards).

## 4. Be honest

**Not allowed:**

- Pretending to be another person, a company, a government official or a public body.
- Scams, phishing, fake job or loan offers, lottery or prize frauds, and requests for one-time codes, PINs or bank details.
- Knowingly spreading false information that could cause real harm, such as fake medical cures, false emergency alerts, or rumours meant to incite violence.
- Fake reviews, fake engagement, or coordinated campaigns that pretend to be independent people.

**Tip:** Samvad will never message you asking for your sign-in code or PIN. Anyone asking for them is trying to take over your account.

## 5. Respect privacy

**Not allowed:**

- Sharing someone's private information — phone number, address, Aadhaar, PAN, bank details, medical information or private photos — without their permission.
- Secretly recording or photographing people in private and sharing it.
- Adding people to groups to harass them, or sharing invite codes to flood a group.

**Remember:** anyone you message can screenshot, forward or export what you send. Share with care.

## 6. No spam

**Not allowed:**

- Bulk or automated messaging, or messaging large numbers of people who have not asked to hear from you.
- Chain messages that pressure people to forward them.
- Unsolicited advertising or promotions.
- Using scripts, bots or modified apps to send messages or scrape information.

Samvad limits how fast accounts can do some actions. Repeatedly hitting those limits, or getting around them, can lead to restrictions.

## 7. No illegal or dangerous activity

**Not allowed:**

- Buying, selling or promoting illegal drugs, weapons, explosives, wildlife products, stolen goods, counterfeit goods or forged documents.
- Promoting terrorism or violent extremism, or praising attacks.
- Encouraging suicide or self-harm. If someone tells you they are in danger, encourage them to seek help; in India you can call **Tele-MANAS on 14416** or **112** in an emergency.
- Unlicensed gambling or betting, or online games that are not permitted under Indian law.
- Malware, viruses, or links designed to hack or steal from people.
- Anything else that breaks the law.

## 8. Respect creators

Do not share content that infringes someone else's copyright or trademark, such as pirated films, music, books or software. See our [Intellectual Property Policy](/legal/intellectual-property) to report infringement.

## 9. Groups

- **Admins** set the tone of their groups. Remove members who break these guidelines and do not create groups for any of the purposes above.
- **Members** are each responsible for what they send in a group.
- Group names, descriptions and photos must follow these guidelines too.

## 10. Your tools

- **Block** anyone from their chat menu. They will not be able to message you or see your online status or profile photo.
- **Report** a person from their chat menu. The report sends us the last 5 messages of that chat so we can review it.
- **Leave** any group at any time.
- **Mute** chats you do not want notifications from.
- Turn on **two-step verification** and **app lock** in Settings.

## 11. How we enforce

We review reports and complaints and act in proportion to the harm:

| Severity | Examples | Typical action |
|---|---|---|
| Severe | Child sexual abuse material, terrorism, credible threats, non-consensual intimate images | Immediate account closure; report to authorities |
| Serious | Scams, impersonation, hate speech, harassment, illegal sales | Content removal and suspension, or closure for repeated breaches |
| Lower | Spam, chain messages, misleading profile | Warning or feature limits; suspension if repeated |

If you think we got it wrong, contact our Grievance Officer through [Grievance Redressal](/legal/grievance-redressal) and we will review it.

## 12. Changes

We update these guidelines as Samvad and the law change. The date at the top shows the current version.
