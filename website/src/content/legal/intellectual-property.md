> **In short.** Do not share content you do not have the rights to. If someone is infringing your copyright or trademark on Samvad, send us a notice with the details below and we will act on it.

## 1. Our policy

We respect intellectual property rights and expect Samvad users to do the same. Our [Terms of Service](/legal/terms-of-service) prohibit sharing content that infringes a patent, trademark, copyright or other proprietary right. We act on valid notices under the Copyright Act, 1957, the Trade Marks Act, 1999, section 79 of the Information Technology Act, 2000 and the IT Rules, 2021, and we close the accounts of repeat infringers.

## 2. Reporting infringement

Send your notice to [{{LEGAL_EMAIL}}](mailto:{{LEGAL_EMAIL}}) with the subject **"IP notice"**. It must include:

1. your name, address, phone number and email, and, if you are an agent, the name of the rights owner you represent;
2. a description of the copyrighted work or registered trademark (with registration number) you claim is infringed;
3. the location of the infringing content on Samvad — for example, the phone number of the account or the name of the group, and a description of the content and when it was shared. Please do not send copies of private messages you are not part of;
4. a statement that you have a good-faith belief that the use is not authorised by the rights owner, its agent or the law;
5. a statement that the information in your notice is accurate, and that you are the rights owner or authorised to act on their behalf;
6. your physical or electronic signature.

For copyright notices under rule 75 of the Copyright Rules, 2013, also include evidence that you own the copyright and, where relevant, the details of any licence.

## 3. What happens next

- We review the notice and may ask for more information.
- If it is valid, we remove or disable access to the content and tell the user who shared it, including a copy or summary of the notice, unless the law prevents this.
- We may restrict or close accounts that repeatedly infringe.

## 4. Counter-notices

If your content was removed and you believe it was a mistake or you have the right to use it, you may send a counter-notice to [{{LEGAL_EMAIL}}](mailto:{{LEGAL_EMAIL}}) with your contact details, a description of the removed content, the reason you believe the removal was wrong, and a statement that the information is accurate. We may share the counter-notice with the person who sent the original notice. Under the Copyright Act, 1957, content removed on a copyright notice may be restored after 21 days unless the complainant shows us a court order to keep it down.

## 5. False notices

Knowingly sending a false notice or counter-notice may expose you to legal liability. Please consider whether a use might be permitted — for example, fair dealing under section 52 of the Copyright Act, 1957 — before sending a notice.

## 6. The Samvad brand

The Samvad name, logo and app design are our intellectual property. You may refer to Samvad by name in news, reviews and commentary. Please do not:

- use our name or logo in a way that suggests we sponsor or endorse you;
- create apps, accounts, groups or websites that look like or impersonate Samvad;
- change our logo or combine it with other marks.
