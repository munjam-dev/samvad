> **In short.** Report problems in the app or email our Grievance Officer. We acknowledge complaints within **24 hours** and resolve them within **15 days**; intimate-image and impersonation complaints are acted on within **24 hours**. If you are not satisfied, you can appeal to the Grievance Appellate Committee or the Data Protection Board of India.

## 2. What you can complain about

- **Content** on Samvad that breaks our [Terms of Service](/legal/terms-of-service), our [Community Guidelines](/legal/community-guidelines) or the law, including any information listed in rule 3(1)(b) of the IT Rules.
- **Your account**, for example if it was restricted or closed, or content you sent was removed, and you think we made a mistake.
- **Your personal data**, for example a request to access, correct or erase it, or a concern about how we handle it (see our [Privacy Policy](/legal/privacy-policy)).
- **Anything else** about the Services.

## 3. How to complain

**Fastest:** for a person or chat, open the chat → **⋮** → **Report** in the app.

**By email** to [{{GRIEVANCE_EMAIL}}](mailto:{{GRIEVANCE_EMAIL}}). To help us act quickly, include:

1. your name and the phone number of your Samvad account (if you have one);
2. what happened, when, and in which chat or group;
3. the phone number of the account involved, if you know it;
4. for intimate-image complaints, a description of the content (please do not send the images themselves);
5. what outcome you are asking for.

**By post:** {{GRIEVANCE_NAME}}, Grievance Officer, {{OPERATOR}}, {{ADDRESS}}.

Complaints can also be made by a person authorised on your behalf.

## 4. Our timelines

| Step | Timeline |
|---|---|
| We acknowledge your complaint | Within **24 hours** |
| We resolve your complaint and tell you the outcome | Within **15 days** of receipt |
| Content that exposes a person's private areas, shows them in full or partial nudity or in a sexual act, or impersonates them, including artificially morphed images | Removed or disabled within **24 hours** of receiving the complaint |
| Requests to remove information listed in rule 3(1)(b) of the IT Rules | Acted on within **72 hours** of the complaint, where applicable |
| Requests about your personal data under the DPDP Act | Within the period set by the DPDP Act rules, and at the latest within **30 days** |

## 5. How we decide

We review each complaint against our Terms, our Community Guidelines and the law. We may ask you for more information. We tell you what we decided and why. If we remove content or restrict an account, we also tell the affected user, unless the law or a safety concern prevents it.

## 6. If you are not satisfied

- **Content and account decisions:** you may appeal to the **Grievance Appellate Committee** constituted by the Government of India under rule 3A of the IT Rules, within 30 days of receiving our decision, through [gac.gov.in](https://gac.gov.in).
- **Personal data complaints:** after using our grievance process, you may complain to the **Data Protection Board of India** under the DPDP Act.
- **Consumer complaints:** you may approach the appropriate consumer commission under the Consumer Protection Act, 2019, or the National Consumer Helpline (**1915**).
- **Crimes:** report to the police or at [cybercrime.gov.in](https://cybercrime.gov.in) (helpline **1930**).

## 7. Court and government orders

We act on orders from a court of competent jurisdiction and on notifications from the appropriate Government or its authorised agency, as rule 3(1)(d) of the IT Rules requires. See our [Law Enforcement Guidelines](/legal/law-enforcement-guidelines).
