> **Samvad has zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE)**, including child sexual abuse material (CSAM), grooming, sextortion and trafficking. If a child is in immediate danger, call **112** now.

## 1. Our commitment

Protecting children is a responsibility we take seriously. These standards describe what we prohibit, how anyone can report it, and what we do when we learn of it. They apply to every part of Samvad: messages, photos, voice notes, files, profiles and groups. They are published to meet the Google Play Child Safety Standards policy and the laws of India.

## 2. Samvad is for adults

- Samvad is only for people **aged 18 and over**. Our [Terms of Service](/legal/terms-of-service) require this.
- We do not knowingly allow children to use Samvad. When we learn that an account belongs to a child, we close it and delete its data.
- We do not show advertising, and we do not target or profile anyone, including children.

## 3. What is prohibited

We prohibit, and remove, any content or behaviour that sexualises, exploits or endangers children, including:

- **CSAM:** any image, video, audio, drawing, computer-generated or morphed image, or text that depicts or describes a child in a sexual way;
- **grooming:** building trust with a child, or with their family, to sexually abuse or exploit them;
- **sextortion:** threatening to share sexual images of a child, or demanding images, money or acts;
- **trafficking:** recruiting, buying, selling or transporting children for exploitation;
- soliciting, offering, sharing or linking to any of the above;
- sexualised comments about children, or content that normalises the abuse of children.

This applies whether the content is real, computer-generated, artificially altered or described in text.

## 4. How to report

### In the app

Open the chat → tap **⋮** → **Report**. Your report reaches our safety team with the reported account and the **last 5 messages** of that chat. You can also **Block** the person. Do not forward, screenshot or download the abusive material yourself; reporting in the app preserves what we need.

### By email

Write to our designated child safety contact at [{{CHILD_SAFETY_EMAIL}}](mailto:{{CHILD_SAFETY_EMAIL}}). Describe what you saw and include the phone number or group involved. **Please do not attach or forward the abusive images or videos.**

### To the authorities in India

- **Emergency:** call **112**.
- **CHILDLINE India:** call **1098** (free, 24 hours).
- **Online:** report at the National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal, [cybercrime.gov.in](https://cybercrime.gov.in), or call **1930**.

## 5. What we do

When we learn of child sexual abuse or exploitation on Samvad, we:

1. **Act fast** — review reports as our highest priority, and act on content showing child sexual abuse within **24 hours**.
2. **Remove** — remove the content and close the accounts involved, which also stops their phone numbers from signing in again.
3. **Preserve** — keep the information needed for an investigation, securely and for as long as the law requires.
4. **Report** — report it to the appropriate authorities, including Indian law enforcement through the National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal, as required by the Protection of Children from Sexual Offences Act, 2012 (section 19) and the Information Technology Act, 2000, and to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) where applicable.
5. **Cooperate** — respond to lawful requests from authorities investigating child exploitation, as set out in our [Law Enforcement Guidelines](/legal/law-enforcement-guidelines).

## 6. How Samvad is designed to reduce harm

- Adults-only eligibility.
- People cannot be messaged by strangers who do not have their phone number or an invite code, and anyone can be blocked instantly.
- In-app reporting on every chat.
- Upload limits on file types and sizes.
- Daily limits on contact lookups and reports, to slow down mass contact and abuse.
- Account closure that also blocks the same phone number from signing in again.

## 7. Compliance and contact

Samvad complies with applicable child safety laws in India, including the Protection of Children from Sexual Offences Act, 2012, the Information Technology Act, 2000 and the Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita, 2023, and with the laws of the other countries where it is available.
